Prototype of DM Tower Defense. 

Summary - You take the role of the Dungeon Master (DM) whose job is to keep his audience engaged with captivating scenarios, daring feats, and moments of petting a dog! Each player has a different rate of losing interest (Attention Span counter above their head) and you must play card (scenarios) to keep them engaged. If any player hits 0 Attention Span, the game is over. This is just a prototype and the additional details are below.
